About the Beta Pilot

What are we testing and why does it matter?

IAWH Health has developed a mobile application designed to support women enrolled in the HeartStrong Community program. The app provides daily self-monitoring support, educational content, health tracking, and community connection, all in one place and built in the IAWH brand identity.

Before the HeartStrong app is finalized, we need real community input. The beta pilot will enroll 20 community volunteers to use the app over a defined testing period and provide structured feedback on usability, design, function, and relevance to their daily lives.

The final version of the HeartStrong app will be built around what beta participants tell us. Your input will directly shape what this app becomes.

What the App Does

Built around how women actually manage their health.

The HeartStrong app is designed as a daily companion for women managing hypertension. Here is what is inside:

♥

Heart Health Check-In

A daily check-in prompt that helps users log their blood pressure readings, note symptoms, and track how they are feeling. Small steps. Big impact. The app makes the daily habit of self-monitoring easy and consistent.

📖

Education

Curated, evidence-based health content on hypertension management, medications, nutrition, stress, and more. Learn. Empower. Thrive. Content is written in plain language and grounded in clinical evidence.

📈

Track Your Health

Visual tracking of blood pressure trends over time, giving users and their clinical teams a clear picture of how numbers are moving. Monitor. Improve. Achieve. Data belongs to the participant.

👥

Community

A shared space for HeartStrong Community participants to connect, share experiences, and support each other. Connect. Share. Support. Built around the understanding that community is medicine.

🌟

Daily Inspiration

A daily prompt designed to reinforce the connection between mindset, motivation, and health behavior. Your health is your greatest wealth. Brief, meaningful, and rooted in the HeartStrong values.

🔔

Session Reminders

For HeartStrong Community participants, the app provides reminders for upcoming virtual sessions, medication schedules, and blood pressure check-in times. Everything in one place, so nothing gets missed.

Scope of the App

What the HeartStrong app is and is not designed to do.

Transparency about scope is essential. The HeartStrong app is a self-management support tool, not a clinical decision-making platform.

What It Is Designed to Do

✓

Support daily blood pressure self-monitoring with easy logging and tracking

✓

Deliver evidence-based health education in plain, accessible language

✓

Visualize blood pressure trends over time for participants and their clinical teams

✓

Connect participants in a peer community for shared support and accountability

✓

Provide daily health inspiration grounded in HeartStrong values

✓

Send reminders for sessions, check-ins, and daily health habits

What It Is Not Designed to Do

✖

Provide medical advice, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations. All clinical decisions remain with your provider.

✖

Replace the HeartStrong Community clinical team. The app supports, not substitutes, clinical care.

✖

Sell or share your data with third parties. Your health information is never a product.

✖

Collect identifiable health information during the beta pilot. All pilot data is de-identified before analysis.

✖

Require prior HeartStrong Community enrollment. The pilot is open to all qualifying community volunteers.

✖

Connect to or sync with external electronic health records or insurance systems during the pilot.

Privacy and Confidentiality

Your privacy is built into the design.

IAWH Health treats the protection of participant data as a foundational commitment, not an afterthought. The following protections are in place for the beta pilot.

🔒

De-Identified Data

All data collected during the beta pilot is de-identified prior to any review or analysis. Your name and personal identifiers are never attached to health or usage data.

📋

Informed Consent

Every beta participant completes an informed consent process before accessing the app. You will understand exactly what data is collected, how it is used, and how to withdraw at any time.

🚫

No Third-Party Sharing

Pilot data is used exclusively for HeartStrong app development and improvement. It is never sold, licensed, or shared with advertisers, insurers, or any external party.

The Evidence Behind Self-Monitoring

Why self-measured blood pressure monitoring works.

The HeartStrong app is built around the practice of self-measured blood pressure monitoring, known as SMBP. This is not a new idea. SMBP has a strong and growing evidence base.

CDC / Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

Self-measured blood pressure monitoring, or SMBP, is when people regularly measure their own blood pressure outside of a clinical setting. It is an evidence-based strategy recommended by the CDC and healthcare organizations to improve blood pressure control, support medication management, and give people a more accurate picture of their cardiovascular health than a single office reading can provide.

Source: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Measure Your Blood Pressure. cdc.gov/bloodpressure

Volunteer Requirements

Who we are looking for.

Beta pilot volunteers should reflect the community HeartStrong is built to serve. Here is what we are looking for in our 20 pilot participants.

  • ✓ An adult woman, 18 years or older, living with hypertension or at elevated cardiovascular risk
  • ✓ All ages and all locations welcome. While we are building community connections in Central Virginia, the beta pilot is open to participants anywhere
  • ✓ Access to a smartphone, either iOS or Android, with the ability to download and use mobile apps
  • ✓ Willingness to use the app regularly over the pilot testing period and to provide structured feedback through brief surveys and a short debrief conversation
  • ✓ Access to a blood pressure monitor, or willingness to obtain one. IAWH Health can provide guidance on selecting a monitor if needed
  • ✓ No prior HeartStrong Community enrollment required. The beta pilot is open to community volunteers regardless of prior program participation
