A National Program. A Local Chapter.

IAWH Health is bringing Walk with a Doc to Central Virginia.

Walk with a Doc is a national nonprofit that has been inspiring communities through movement and conversation since 2005. Founded by Dr. David Sabgir, a cardiologist in Columbus, Ohio, it has grown to over 700 chapters and millions of participants in communities across the country and around the world.

IAWH Health is developing a Walk with a Doc affiliate chapter in Central Virginia, beginning in Petersburg, the only Walk with a Doc chapter in the Tri-Cities region. Our chapter will bring together community members and local healthcare providers for free guided walks that combine physical activity, practical health education, and the kind of social connection that research consistently links to better health outcomes.

Community partnership development is actively underway. If you are a healthcare provider, community organization, faith community, or local business interested in being part of this launch, we want to hear from you.

Why Walk with a Doc Works

Four things that make every walk more than a walk.

Walk with a Doc chapters offer something more than exercise. Four components work together to make each event a complete prescription for a healthier life.

01

Physical Activity

Regular walking is one of the single most important things we can do for both physical and mental health. It benefits the heart, brain, bones, and lungs, and helps prevent chronic diseases including hypertension, diabetes, and certain cancers. You do not need to be fast. You just need to move.

02

Health Education

Every Walk with a Doc event begins with a brief discussion on a current health topic led by a licensed healthcare provider. Then questions get answered while the group walks together. The result is reliable, practical health information delivered in a relaxed, judgment-free setting.

03

Social Connection

Research consistently shows that building connections with others improves quality of life, boosts mood, and reduces the risk of depression and isolation. Walk with a Doc events are a way to meet new people, spend time with neighbors, and share a common goal: walking together for better health.

04

Time in Nature

Spending time outdoors has a measurable impact on health and well-being. Research links time in nature to reduced stress, depression, and anxiety, and improved sleep, brain function, and immune response. Walk with a Doc events take place outdoors whenever possible. Consider it a prescription for Vitamin N.
